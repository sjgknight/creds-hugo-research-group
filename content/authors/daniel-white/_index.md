Daniel White is a Lecturer in Psychology whose research interests include AI in education, research and work, particularly the human and behavioural factors that shape how people engage with emerging technologies. He is interested not only in what technologies such as AI can do, but in how people actually adopt them, how they affect teaching and learning, and how they are changing professional practice. He is also working around curriculum innovation, authentic assessment and employability, and is keen to develop further research from this side of his teaching practice.
