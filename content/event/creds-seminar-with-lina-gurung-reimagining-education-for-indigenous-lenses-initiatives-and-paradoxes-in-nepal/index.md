title: "CREDS Seminar with Lina Gurung: Reimagining Education for Indigenous
  Lenses: Initiatives and Paradoxes in Nepal"
abstract: Lina Gurung is a Nepali scholar with Gurung heritage. Her PhD was on
  experiences of online and distance education learners in Nepal, and she has
  worked as a gender coordinator in a project enhancing access of teacher
  professional development using ICT. Her interests in digital pedagogy, digital
  divides and decolonial education will inform her talk exploring how to
  integrate and sustain initiatives in education from Indigenous perspectives.
  Lina will highlight paradoxes encountered in concrete work undertaken at
  Kathmandu University School of Education.
